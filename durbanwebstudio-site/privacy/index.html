Privacy notice

Last updated: September 2026

Who we are

Durban Web Studio is a small web design business in Durban, South Africa.

What we collect

Only what you type into a form on this site:

  • your name and email address
  • details about your request, such as an event date, number of guests, room, service or message

Why we collect it

Only to reply to you. We do not sell your details, and we do not use them for anything else.

The example websites

The example websites (the salon, the caterer and the guesthouse) are made-up businesses. If you use their forms, your details come to Durban Web Studio and are handled the same way as this notice describes.

How your details travel

When you send a form, it goes through a service called Web3Forms, which passes it on to our email inbox. Web3Forms may keep a copy for a short time. See the Web3Forms website for its own privacy policy.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use cookies, advertising or analytics. The pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google to download them.

Your choices

You can ask to see the details we hold about you, or ask us to delete them. Use the contact form on the home page and say what you would like.
